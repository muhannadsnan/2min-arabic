Arabic Extras — in Syria and much of the Arab world, numbers are written with Arabic digits. Learn to read one to ten in about a minute, and watch out for the three traps: five looks like a zero, six looks like a seven, and seven and eight are just a V.

Two minutes a day. Your first Arabic conversation in 30 days — this is a bonus lesson after Day 10.

🔢 The digits:
1 — waaHid — ⁧١ وَاحِدْ⁩
2 — ithnaan — ⁧٢ اِثْنَانْ⁩
3 — thalaatha — ⁧٣ ثَلَاثَةْ⁩
4 — arba'a — ⁧٤ أَرْبَعَةْ⁩
5 — khamsa — ⁧٥ خَمْسَةْ⁩
6 — sitta — ⁧٦ سِتَّةْ⁩
7 — sab'a — ⁧٧ سَبْعَةْ⁩
8 — thamaaniya — ⁧٨ ثَمَانِيَةْ⁩
9 — tis'a — ⁧٩ تِسْعَةْ⁩
10 — 'ashara — ⁧١٠ عَشَرَةْ⁩
0 is just a dot — ⁧٠⁩
