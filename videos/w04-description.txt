📝 Some of what you'll learn:
qahwa ma'a Haliib — coffee with milk — ⁧قَهْوَةْ مَعَ حَلِيبْ⁩
bikam haadhaa? — how much is this? — ⁧بِكَمْ هَذَا؟⁩
ash-shaay — the tea — ⁧الشَّايْ⁩
ayna? mataa? — where? when? — ⁧أَيْنَ؟ مَتَى؟⁩
yamiin, thumma yasaar — right, then left — ⁧يَمِينْ، ثُمَّ يَسَارْ⁩
uHibbu dimashq! — I love Damascus! — ⁧أُحِبُّ دِمَشْقْ!⁩
