Day 11 of 30 — a tourist stops Lina on a street in Damascus. Can you follow the whole conversation? You already know almost every word.

Two minutes a day. Your first Arabic conversation in 30 days.

🆕 New words:
ahlan wa sahlan — welcome — ⁧أَهْلًا وَسَهْلًا⁩
saa'iH — a tourist — ⁧سَائِحْ⁩
'afwan — excuse me (also: you're welcome) — ⁧عَفْوًا⁩
dimashqu jamiila — Damascus is beautiful — ⁧دِمَشْقُ جَمِيلَةْ⁩

💬 The conversation:
'afwan, marhaban! — Excuse me, hello! — ⁧عَفْوًا، مَرْحَبًا!⁩
ana saa'iH. ana min amriikaa. — I'm a tourist. I'm from America. — ⁧أَنَا سَائِحْ. أَنَا مِنْ أَمْرِيكَا.⁩
ana Liinaa. ana min lubnaan. — I'm Lina. I'm from Lebanon. — ⁧أَنَا لِينَا. أَنَا مِنْ لُبْنَانْ.⁩
tasharrafnaa. ismii Tom. — Nice to meet you. My name is Tom. — ⁧تَشَرَّفْنَا. اسْمِي تُومْ.⁩
kayfa Haaluk yaa Tom? — How are you, Tom? — ⁧كَيْفَ حَالُكْ يَا تُومْ؟⁩
bikhayr, shukran. dimashqu jamiila! — Fine, thanks. Damascus is beautiful! — ⁧بِخَيْرْ، شُكْرًا. دِمَشْقُ جَمِيلَةْ!⁩
