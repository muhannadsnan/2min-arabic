📝 Some of what you'll learn:
na'am / laa — yes / no — ⁧نَعَمْ / لَا⁩
aasif — sorry — ⁧آسِفْ⁩
Tayyib — okay, good — ⁧طَيِّبْ⁩
min ayna anta? — where are you from? — ⁧مِنْ أَيْنَ أَنْتَ؟⁩
ana min … — I am from … — ⁧أَنَا مِنْ⁩
mu'allim / mu'allima — teacher (man / woman) — ⁧مُعَلِّمْ / مُعَلِّمَةْ⁩
waaHid … 'ashara — 1 … 10 — ⁧وَاحِدْ … عَشَرَةْ⁩
