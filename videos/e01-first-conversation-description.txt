Your first real conversation in Arabic — hello, how are you, your name — and then a tiny story about Sami and his coffee that uses the same words. From Episode 1, the full course from zero.

📝 Some of what you'll learn:
as-salaamu 'alaykum — hello (peace be upon you) — ⁧السَّلَامُ عَلَيْكُمْ⁩
wa-'alaykumu s-salaam — and peace be upon you (the answer) — ⁧وَعَلَيْكُمُ السَّلَامْ⁩
kayfa Haaluk? — how are you? — ⁧كَيْفَ حَالُكْ؟⁩
bikhayr, al-Hamdu lillaah — fine, thank God — ⁧بِخَيْرْ، الْحَمْدُ لِلَّهْ⁩
saamii sa'iidun jiddan — Sami is very happy — ⁧سَامِي سَعِيدٌ جِدًّا⁩
