Day 6 of 30 — yes, no, sorry, okay: the little Arabic words that make you sound polite.

Two minutes a day. Your first Arabic conversation in 30 days.

🆕 New words:
na'am — yes — ⁧نَعَمْ⁩
laa — no — ⁧لَا⁩
aasif / aasifa — sorry (man / woman) — ⁧آسِفْ / آسِفَةْ⁩
laa ba's — no problem — ⁧لَا بَأْسْ⁩
Tayyib — okay, good — ⁧طَيِّبْ⁩
tamaam — perfect, all good — ⁧تَمَامْ⁩

📝 Today's sentences:
na'am, min faDlik — Yes, please. — ⁧نَعَمْ، مِنْ فَضْلِكْ⁩
laa, shukran — No, thank you. — ⁧لَا، شُكْرًا⁩
tamaam, al-Hamdu lillaah — All good, thank God. — ⁧تَمَامْ، الْحَمْدُ لِلَّهْ⁩
