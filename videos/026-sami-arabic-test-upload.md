```
Day 26 of 30 — an easy Arabic story: Sami is scared of tomorrow's test, so Lina asks him the 3 questions every Arab will ask you: your age, where you live and what you study.

Two minutes a day. Your first Arabic conversation in 30 days.

🆕 New words:
imtiHaan — test, exam — ⁧امْتِحَانْ⁩
Sa'b — hard, difficult — ⁧صَعْبْ⁩
sahl — easy — ⁧سَهْلْ⁩
mumtaaz — excellent! — ⁧مُمْتَازْ⁩

❓ The 3 questions every Arab asks:
kam 'umruk? — How old are you? — ⁧كَمْ عُمْرُكْ؟⁩
'umrii 'ishruuna sana. — I'm twenty years old. — ⁧عُمْرِي عِشْرُونَ سَنَةْ.⁩
ayna taskunu? — Where do you live? — ⁧أَيْنَ تَسْكُنُ؟⁩
askunu fii dimashq. — I live in Damascus. — ⁧أَسْكُنُ فِي دِمَشْقْ.⁩
maadhaa tadrusu? — What do you study? — ⁧مَاذَا تَدْرُسُ؟⁩
adrusu l-'arabiyya! — I study Arabic! — ⁧أَدْرُسُ الْعَرَبِيَّةْ!⁩

📖 The story:
ghadan imtiHaan… Sa'b! — Tomorrow is the test… it's hard! — ⁧غَدًا امْتِحَانْ… صَعْبْ!⁩
laa, sahl! — No, it's easy! — ⁧لَا، سَهْلْ!⁩
haadhaa huwa l-imtiHaan! — That WAS the test! — ⁧هَذَا هُوَ الِامْتِحَانْ!⁩
al-imtiHaan sahl! — The test is easy! — ⁧الِامْتِحَانْ سَهْلْ!⁩
```
