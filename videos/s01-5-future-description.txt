The future tense in Arabic is easy: put "sa" in front of the verb. The story of the horse and the turtle, told again as tomorrow: goes → will go, drinks → will drink.

📝 Words from this part:
yadh-hab → sa-yadh-hab — goes → will go — ⁧يذهب ← سيذهب⁩
yashrab → sa-yashrab — drinks → will drink — ⁧يشرب ← سيشرب⁩
yanaam → sa-yanaam — sleeps → will sleep — ⁧ينام ← سينام⁩
laa yanaam → lan yanaam — doesn't sleep → won't sleep — ⁧لا ينام ← لن ينام⁩
ghadan — tomorrow — ⁧غدا⁩
