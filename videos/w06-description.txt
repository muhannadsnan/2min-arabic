📝 Some of what you'll learn:
kam 'umruk? — how old are you? — ⁧كَمْ عُمْرُكْ؟⁩
ayna taskunu? — where do you live? — ⁧أَيْنَ تَسْكُنُ؟⁩
hal tatakallamu l-'arabiyya? — do you speak Arabic? — ⁧هَلْ تَتَكَلَّمُ الْعَرَبِيَّةْ؟⁩
na'am, qaliilan — yes, a little — ⁧نَعَمْ، قَلِيلًا⁩
hal anti Taaliba? — are you a student? (to a woman) — ⁧هَلْ أَنْتِ طَالِبَةْ؟⁩
mabruuk! — congratulations! — ⁧مَبْرُوكْ!⁩
