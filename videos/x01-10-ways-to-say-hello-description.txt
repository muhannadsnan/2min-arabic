Arabic Extras — 10 ways to say hello in Arabic: 5 in standard Arabic (fusha), 5 the way Syrians really say it.

Two minutes a day. Your first Arabic conversation in 30 days — this is a bonus lesson.

📘 Standard Arabic (understood everywhere):
1. as-salaamu 'alaykum — Peace be upon you (hello) — ⁧السَّلَامُ عَلَيْكُمْ⁩
2. marhaban — Hello — ⁧مَرْحَبًا⁩
3. ahlan wa sahlan — Welcome — ⁧أَهْلًا وَسَهْلًا⁩
4. sabaah al-khayr — Good morning — ⁧صَبَاحُ الْخَيْرْ⁩
5. masaa' al-khayr — Good evening — ⁧مَسَاءُ الْخَيْرْ⁩

🇸🇾 Syrian dialect (everyday speech in Syria):
6. ahleen — Hi — ⁧أَهْلِين⁩
7. marhabtein — Two hellos (hi there) — ⁧مَرْحَبْتَين⁩
8. hala wallah — Hey, so good to see you — ⁧هَلَا وَاللهْ⁩
9. yis'id sabaahak — Have a happy morning — ⁧يِسْعِدْ صَبَاحَكْ⁩
10. kiifak? — How are you? (to a man) — ⁧كِيفَكْ؟⁩
