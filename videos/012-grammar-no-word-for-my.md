# Day 12 — Grammar: Arabic Has No Word for "My"

| | |
|---|---|
| **Format** | Grammar (with a little book argument between Sami and Lina) |

| **Goal** | The viewer adds ـِي (my) and ـُكَ / ـُكِ (your, to a man / a woman) to a word, knows that the soft ة becomes a "t" before the ending (قَهْوَة → قَهْوَتِي), and connects it to مَا اسْمُكْ؟ from Day 2. |
