Day 20 of 30 — four Arabic verbs: I drink, I eat, I go, I love, and the one letter that means "I".

Two minutes a day. Your first Arabic conversation in 30 days.

🆕 New verbs (the alif at the start = I):
ashrabu — I drink — ⁧أَشْرَبُ⁩
aakulu — I eat — ⁧آكُلُ⁩
adh-habu — I go — ⁧أَذْهَبُ⁩
uHibbu — I love / I like — ⁧أُحِبُّ⁩

📝 Today's sentences:
ashrabu sh-shaay. — I drink tea. — ⁧أَشْرَبُ الشَّايْ.⁩
aakulu ka'ka. — I eat cake. — ⁧آكُلُ كَعْكَةْ.⁩
adh-habu ilaa l-maqhaa. — I go to the café. — ⁧أَذْهَبُ إِلَى الْمَقْهَى.⁩
uHibbu l-qahwa. — I love coffee. — ⁧أُحِبُّ الْقَهْوَةْ.⁩
uHibbu dimashq! — I love Damascus! — ⁧أُحِبُّ دِمَشْقْ!⁩
