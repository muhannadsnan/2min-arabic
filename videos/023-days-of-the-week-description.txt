Day 23 of 30 — the days of the week in Arabic, and the trick that makes five of them easy: they are the numbers one to five.

Two minutes a day. Your first Arabic conversation in 30 days.

📅 The days (the week starts on Sunday):
al-aHad — Sunday (1, waaHid) — ⁧الْأَحَدْ⁩
al-ithnayn — Monday (2, ithnaan) — ⁧الِاثْنَيْنْ⁩
ath-thulaathaa' — Tuesday (3, thalaatha) — ⁧الثُّلَاثَاءْ⁩
al-arbi'aa' — Wednesday (4, arba'a) — ⁧الْأَرْبِعَاءْ⁩
al-khamiis — Thursday (5, khamsa) — ⁧الْخَمِيسْ⁩
al-jumu'a — Friday — ⁧الْجُمُعَةْ⁩
as-sabt — Saturday — ⁧السَّبْتْ⁩

🗓️ Time words:
yawm — day — ⁧يَوْمْ⁩
al-yawm — today — ⁧الْيَوْمْ⁩
ghadan — tomorrow — ⁧غَدًا⁩
al-yawm al-aHad. — Today is Sunday. — ⁧الْيَوْمْ الْأَحَدْ.⁩
ghadan al-jumu'a. — Tomorrow is Friday. — ⁧غَدًا الْجُمُعَةْ.⁩
