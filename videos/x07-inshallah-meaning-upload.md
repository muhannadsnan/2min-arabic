## Description

```
What does inshallah really mean? Word by word it means "if God wills" — but Arabs use it in three different ways, and the tone tells you which one.

🆕 The word:
in shaa' allaah — if God wills — ⁧إِنْ شَاءَ اللهْ⁩
in — if — ⁧إِنْ⁩ · shaa' — wills — ⁧شَاءَ⁩ · allaah — God — ⁧اللهْ⁩

💬 The 3 ways:
1. Warm and hopeful — "I really hope so!"
2. Slow and polite — "maybe… we'll see."
3. A normal yes to a plan:
naltaqii ghadan? — Shall we meet tomorrow? — ⁧نَلْتَقِي غَدًا؟⁩
in shaa' allaah. — Yes, God willing. — ⁧إِنْ شَاءَ اللهْ.⁩
```
