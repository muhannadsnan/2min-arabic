Arabic Vocab — 10 colors in Arabic and English, each on a real thing from a Damascus market. The first six share one pattern, and four are named after things.

🎨 The 10 colors:
aHmar — red — ⁧أَحْمَرْ⁩
azraq — blue — ⁧أَزْرَقْ⁩
akhDar — green — ⁧أَخْضَرْ⁩
aSfar — yellow — ⁧أَصْفَرْ⁩
abyaD — white — ⁧أَبْيَضْ⁩
aswad — black — ⁧أَسْوَدْ⁩
bunnii — brown — ⁧بُنِّيّ⁩
burtuqaalii — orange — ⁧بُرْتُقَالِيّ⁩
wardii — pink — ⁧وَرْدِيّ⁩
ramaadii — grey — ⁧رَمَادِيّ⁩

💡 The trick: the color = the thing + -ii
bunn (coffee beans) → bunnii, brown — ⁧بُنّ ← بُنِّيّ⁩
burtuqaal (an orange) → burtuqaalii, orange — ⁧بُرْتُقَال ← بُرْتُقَالِيّ⁩
ward (a rose) → wardii, pink — ⁧وَرْد ← وَرْدِيّ⁩
ramaad (ash) → ramaadii, grey — ⁧رَمَاد ← رَمَادِيّ⁩
