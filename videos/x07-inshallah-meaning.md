### 🎬 Scene 4 — Way 1: I really hope so

🖼️ **Image:** Sami on the rooftop, hopeful
🔤 **On screen:** إِنْ شَاءَ اللهْ! · *in shaa' allaah!* · I really hope so! · Way 1/3


### 🎬 Scene 5 — Way 2: we'll see

🖼️ **Image:** Sami on the rooftop, a polite small smile
🔤 **On screen:** إِنْ شَاءَ اللهْ · *in shaa' allaah…* · Maybe… we'll see. · Way 2/3

```say:narrator

```
```say:teacher

```
