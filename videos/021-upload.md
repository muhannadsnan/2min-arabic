Day 21 of 30 — a real café challenge: a waiter in Damascus asks you five questions. Can you answer them all in Arabic before Sami does?

☕ What the waiter asks:
ahlan wa sahlan! — Welcome! — ⁧أَهْلًا وَسَهْلًا!⁩
hal turiidu qahwa? — Do you want coffee? — ⁧هَلْ تُرِيدُ قَهْوَةْ؟⁩
sukkar? — Sugar? — ⁧سُكَّرْ؟⁩
kam finjaanan? — How many cups? — ⁧كَمْ فِنْجَانًا؟⁩
tafaDDal — Here you go. — ⁧تَفَضَّلْ⁩

laa, shukran. uriidu shaay, min faDlik. — No thanks. I want tea, please. — ⁧لَا، شُكْرًا. أُرِيدُ شَايْ، مِنْ فَضْلِكْ.⁩
laa, biduuni sukkar. — No, without sugar. — ⁧لَا، بِدُونِ سُكَّرْ.⁩
thalaatha. — Three. — ⁧ثَلَاثَةْ.⁩
shukran. bikam haadhaa? — Thank you. How much is this? — ⁧شُكْرًا. بِكَمْ هَذَا؟⁩
