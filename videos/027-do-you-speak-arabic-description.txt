Day 27 of 30 — "Do you speak Arabic?" is the first question every Arab will ask you. Here's the perfect two-word answer, how to say you speak English, and how to ask it back.

Two minutes a day. Your first Arabic conversation in 30 days.

🆕 New phrases:
hal tatakallamu l-'arabiyya? — Do you speak Arabic? — ⁧هَلْ تَتَكَلَّمُ الْعَرَبِيَّةْ؟⁩
na'am, qaliilan. — Yes, a little. — ⁧نَعَمْ، قَلِيلًا.⁩
atakallamu l-ingliiziyya. — I speak English. — ⁧أَتَكَلَّمُ الْإِنْجِلِيزِيَّةْ.⁩
hal tatakallamu l-ingliiziyya? — Do you speak English? — ⁧هَلْ تَتَكَلَّمُ الْإِنْجِلِيزِيَّةْ؟⁩

🛟 The lifesaver (Day 22):
laa afham. bi-buT', min faDlik. — I don't understand. Slowly, please. — ⁧لَا أَفْهَمْ. بِبُطْءْ، مِنْ فَضْلِكْ.⁩

🗣️ Syrian bonus:
shwayy shwayy — little by little — ⁧شْوَيّ شْوَيّ⁩
