Day 13 of 30 — Lina shows Sami her family photos. A short story in Arabic, with a surprise at the end.

Two minutes a day. Your first Arabic conversation in 30 days.

🆕 New words:
ab — father — ⁧أَبْ⁩
umm — mother — ⁧أُمّ⁩
akh — brother — ⁧أَخْ⁩
ukht — sister — ⁧أُخْتْ⁩
'aa'ila — family — ⁧عَائِلَةْ⁩
haadhihi — this (for a woman or girl) — ⁧هَذِهِ⁩
qiTT — cat — ⁧قِطّ⁩

📖 The story:
haadhihi 'aa'ilatii. — This is my family. — ⁧هَذِهِ عَائِلَتِي.⁩
haadhaa abii. — This is my father. — ⁧هَذَا أَبِي.⁩
haadhihi ummii. ummii mu'allima. — This is my mother. My mother is a teacher. — ⁧هَذِهِ أُمِّي. أُمِّي مُعَلِّمَةْ.⁩
haadhaa akhii. huwa Taalib. — This is my brother. He is a student. — ⁧هَذَا أَخِي. هُوَ طَالِبْ.⁩
wa haadhihi ukhtii. hiya sa'iida. — And this is my sister. She is happy. — ⁧وَهَذِهِ أُخْتِي. هِيَ سَعِيدَةْ.⁩
haadhaa qiTTii! — This is my cat! — ⁧هَذَا قِطِّي!⁩
