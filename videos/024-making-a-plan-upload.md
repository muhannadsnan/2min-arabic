Day 24 of 30 — make a plan in Arabic: Sami invites Lina for coffee tomorrow, after the lesson… but tomorrow is Friday!

Two minutes a day. Your first Arabic conversation in 30 days.

🆕 New words:
ba'da — after — ⁧بَعْدَ⁩
idhan — so, then — ⁧إِذًا⁩
hayyaa! — let's go! — ⁧هَيَّا!⁩

💬 The conversation:
hal turiidiina qahwa ghadan? — Do you want coffee tomorrow? (to a woman) — ⁧هَلْ تُرِيدِينَ قَهْوَةْ غَدًا؟⁩
ghadan? mataa? — Tomorrow? When? — ⁧غَدًا؟ مَتَى؟⁩
ghadan, ba'da d-dars. — Tomorrow, after the lesson. — ⁧غَدًا، بَعْدَ الدَّرْسْ.⁩
ghadan al-jumu'a… laa dars! — Tomorrow is Friday… no lesson! — ⁧غَدًا الْجُمُعَةْ… لَا دَرْسْ!⁩
idhan… al-aan? — So… now? — ⁧إِذًا… الْآنْ؟⁩
Tayyib, hayyaa! — Okay, let's go! — ⁧طَيِّبْ، هَيَّا!⁩
