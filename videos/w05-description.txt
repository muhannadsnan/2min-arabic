📝 Some of what you'll learn:
laa, biduuni sukkar — no, without sugar — ⁧لَا، بِدُونِ سُكَّرْ⁩
bi-buT', min faDlik — slowly, please — ⁧بِبُطْءْ، مِنْ فَضْلِكْ⁩
laa afham — I don't understand — ⁧لَا أَفْهَمْ⁩
al-yawm al-aHad — today is Sunday — ⁧الْيَوْمْ الْأَحَدْ⁩
hayyaa! — let's go! — ⁧هَيَّا!⁩
hal anta Taalib? — are you a student? — ⁧هَلْ أَنْتَ طَالِبْ؟⁩
