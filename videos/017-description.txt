Day 17 of 30 — the word "the" in Arabic is al-, but with sun letters the L disappears. Can you hear it?

Two minutes a day. Your first Arabic conversation in 30 days.

🆕 The rule:
al- — the — ⁧الْ⁩

🌙 Moon letters (you hear the L):
al-qahwa — the coffee — ⁧الْقَهْوَةْ⁩
al-kitaab — the book — ⁧الْكِتَابْ⁩
al-bayt — the house — ⁧الْبَيْتْ⁩
al-Haliib — the milk — ⁧الْحَلِيبْ⁩
al-'aSiir — the juice — ⁧الْعَصِيرْ⁩

☀️ Sun letters (the L disappears):
ash-shaay — the tea — ⁧الشَّايْ⁩
as-sukkar — the sugar — ⁧السُّكَّرْ⁩
an-naadil — the waiter — ⁧النَّادِلْ⁩
