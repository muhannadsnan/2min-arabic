Day 29 of 30 — the same conversation as Day 28, but now you are the woman: talking to a woman, a few small sounds change.

Two minutes a day. Your first Arabic conversation in 30 days.

💬 The conversation (you are Lina):
kayfa Haaluki? — How are you? (to a woman) — ⁧كَيْفَ حَالُكِ؟⁩
bikhayr, al-Hamdu lillaah. wa-anta? — Fine, thank God. And you? (to a man) — ⁧بِخَيْرْ، الْحَمْدُ لِلَّهْ. وَأَنْتَ؟⁩
maa smuki? — What's your name? (to a woman) — ⁧مَا اسْمُكِ؟⁩
ismii liinaa. — My name is Lina. — ⁧اسْمِي لِينَا.⁩
min ayna anti? — Where are you from? (to a woman) — ⁧مِنْ أَيْنَ أَنْتِ؟⁩
ana min lubnaan. — I'm from Lebanon. — ⁧أَنَا مِنْ لُبْنَانْ.⁩
hal anti Taaliba? — Are you a student? (to a woman) — ⁧هَلْ أَنْتِ طَالِبَةْ؟⁩
na'am, ana Taaliba. — Yes, I'm a student. (a woman) — ⁧نَعَمْ، أَنَا طَالِبَةْ.⁩
hal turiidiina qahwa? — Do you want coffee? (to a woman) — ⁧هَلْ تُرِيدِينَ قَهْوَةْ؟⁩
laa, uriidu shaay! — No, I want tea! — ⁧لَا، أُرِيدُ شَايْ!⁩
