Day 18 of 30 — five Arabic question words: what, who, where, how many and when. Lina asks, Sami answers.

Two minutes a day. Your first Arabic conversation in 30 days.

🆕 Question words:
maa — what — ⁧مَا⁩
man — who — ⁧مَنْ⁩
ayna — where — ⁧أَيْنَ⁩
kam — how many — ⁧كَمْ⁩
mataa — when — ⁧مَتَى⁩

📝 Questions and answers:
maa haadhaa? — kitaabii — What is this? — My book. — ⁧مَا هَذَا؟ — كِتَابِي⁩
man haadhihi? — haadhihi ukhtii. — Who is this? — This is my sister. — ⁧مَنْ هَذِهِ؟ — هَذِهِ أُخْتِي.⁩
ayna l-qiTT? — hunaa! — Where is the cat? — Here! — ⁧أَيْنَ الْقِطّ؟ — هُنَا!⁩
kam finjaanan? — thalaatha. — How many cups? — Three. — ⁧كَمْ فِنْجَانًا؟ — ثَلَاثَةْ.⁩
mataa d-dars? — al-aan! — When is the lesson? — Now! — ⁧مَتَى الدَّرْسْ؟ — الْآنْ!⁩
