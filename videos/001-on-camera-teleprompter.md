# Day 1 — On-camera version (teleprompter)

Same content as [001-why-2-minutes.md](001-why-2-minutes.md), rewritten to be **spoken by you on camera**.
Short lines = natural breathing points. Say it your way — if a sentence feels unnatural in your mouth, change it.

## Filming setup (5 minutes)

- **Phone vertical (9:16)**, at eye level (books under the phone), about 1 m away, frame from mid-chest up.
- **Face a window** (daylight on your face, never behind you). Quiet room; switch off fans/AC.
- **Sound matters more than picture:** if you have wired earphones with a mic or a clip-on mic, use it. Otherwise keep the phone close.
- **Look into the lens**, not at the screen. Smile at the start.
- Film **each part separately** (A, B, C…) and do 2–3 takes of each. Pause 2 seconds before and after each take (easy to cut).
- It's fine to read from a teleprompter app (e.g. a free "teleprompter" app on a second phone/laptop placed right behind the camera).

`[IMAGE: …]` = I'll lay a picture over your video at that moment (you keep talking; viewers still hear you).
`[TEXT: …]` = on-screen text card I'll generate.

---

### A — Hook (≈5 s) · energetic, straight into the lens

> Can you find two minutes a day?
> Then you can speak Arabic.
> Let me tell you why I'm so sure.

`[TEXT: Can you find 2 minutes a day?]`

### B — Welcome (≈12 s) · warm, relaxed

> Welcome to 2 Minute Arabic —
> one tiny lesson, every day.
> Arabic is my mother tongue,
> and my promise is simple:
> two minutes a day,
> and in thirty days you'll have your first conversation in Arabic.

`[TEXT: 2 Minute Arabic · Day 1]`

### C — Today (≈6 s)

> Today is Day 1.
> No grammar yet —
> just my story, one idea that changed how I learn,
> and your very first Arabic word.

### D — My story (≈30 s) · honest, a bit self-ironic

> Spanish is my fourth language.
> I've been learning it for two years.
> But I learned it the wrong way.
>
> One month, I'd go all in —
> notes, videos, podcasts, hours of conversations.
> `[IMAGE: motivated learner at night surrounded by notes]`
>
> Then life got busy…
> and I wouldn't touch Spanish for two months.
> `[IMAGE: dusty notebooks, calendar pages flying]`
>
> Then another burst. Then another break.
>
> Two years later, I can finally hold a simple conversation with a native speaker.
> Two years… for level A2.
> `[TEXT: Spanish = my 4th language · 2 years · A2]`

### E — The realization (≈10 s) · slower, sincere

> And here's the painful part.
> If I had given Spanish just two minutes, every single day,
> I'd be much further today.
>
> Two minutes isn't magic.
> *Every day* is.

### F — The math (≈20 s) · a little playful

> Here's the math.
> Get one percent better every day,
> and after one year you're not three hundred sixty-five percent better.
> You're about *thirty-eight times* better.
> `[TEXT: 1.01³⁶⁵ ≈ 37.8 📈]`
>
> Now slip one percent every day…
> and after a year, you're close to zero.
> `[TEXT: 0.99³⁶⁵ ≈ 0.03 📉]`
>
> Small things, done daily, compound.

### G — The promise (≈12 s) · firm, point at the camera if it feels natural

> So don't make my mistake.
> Don't wait for the perfect month — it never comes.
> Give me two minutes a day,
> and in thirty days you'll start your first conversation in Arabic.
> No BS. No excuses.

### H — First word (≈15 s) · teacher mode, slow and clear

> Let's start right now. Your first word:
> **مَرْحَبًا** — marhaban.
> It means *hello*.
> Your turn — say it out loud.
> *(wait 2 seconds, smile, nod)*
> **مَرْحَبًا.**
> *(wait 2 seconds)*

`[TEXT: مَرْحَبًا · marhaban · Hello]` + "🗣️ Your turn!" during the pauses

### I — Outro (≈12 s) · warm, wave at the end

> Day 1 — done.
> Tomorrow: the ten most useful phrases in Arabic.
> That's your two minutes for today.
> Come back tomorrow — same place, two minutes.
> Subscribe so you don't break your streak.
> **مَعَ السَّلَامَة!**

`[TEXT: مَعَ السَّلَامَة · ma'a s-salaama · Goodbye — Day 1 ✅]`
