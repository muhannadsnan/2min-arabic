One hour of beginner Arabic, repeated 8 times. Close your eyes and listen: first the English, then the Arabic, spoken by a real Arab. Every 5 phrases you hear them again (Arabic first, try to remember the meaning), so each phrase comes back 4 times an hour. Rounds 1–2 at a normal pace, rounds 3–8 slower and quieter, and the screen stays dark.

234 basic Arabic phrases in 10 topics: greetings, introducing yourself, questions and answers, polite words, numbers 1 to 20 and the days, family and jobs, the café and food, everyday verbs, finding your way, and a short story.
