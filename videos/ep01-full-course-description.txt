All Arabic is spoken by native speakers. Every word on screen with transliteration and English.

📝 Some of what you'll learn:
marhaban — hello — ⁧مَرْحَبًا⁩
ismii … — my name is … — ⁧اسْمِي …⁩
ana min suuriyaa — I'm from Syria — ⁧أَنَا مِنْ سُورِيَا⁩
uriidu shaay, min faDlik — I'd like tea, please — ⁧أُرِيدُ شَايْ، مِنْ فَضْلِكْ⁩
ayna l-maqhaa? — where is the café? — ⁧أَيْنَ الْمَقْهَى؟⁩
uHibbu dimashq! — I love Damascus! — ⁧أُحِبُّ دِمَشْقْ!⁩
