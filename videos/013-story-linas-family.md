# Day 13 — Story: Lina's Family

| | |
|---|---|
| **Format** | Story (Lina shows Sami a family photo album — told in Lina's own voice) |
| **Target length** | ~2:20 |
| **Goal** | The viewer understands a short story told only in Arabic, knows mother / father / brother / sister / family, uses هَذَا (a man) and هَذِهِ (a woman), and uses yesterday's "my" (أُمِّي، أَبِي). |
| **New words** | أُمّ (mother) · أَبْ (father) · أَخْ (brother) · أُخْتْ (sister) · عَائِلَةْ (family) · هَذِهِ (this, for a woman) · قِطّ (cat) |
| **Re-used (due)** | هَذَا (Day 4) · ـِي my, ـُكِ your (Day 12) · مُعَلِّمَةْ · طَالِبْ (Day 9) · سَعِيدَةْ (Day 5) · جَمِيلْ (Day 11) · كِتَابِي (Day 12, warm-up) |
| **Voices** | English narrator = owner's clone · Arabic = Koki (Lina, the storyteller) + the owner (teacher, Sami) → **AI label: No** |
| **Look** | 3D · new characters: Lina's father (fifties, grey-flecked hair, neat beard, glasses, cardigan), mother (fifties, kind face, light headscarf, long loose dress), brother (teen, curly hair, hoodie, backpack), sister (about 10, ponytail, long loose dress) and a fluffy grey cat · opener: Lina, close-up, holds up a framed family photo beside her face (not in front of it) with a warm smile and holds (new opener, owner approves) |
| **YouTube title** | `Meet Lina's Family (A Short Story in Arabic) \| Day 13 · 2 Minute Arabic` |
| **Thumbnail text** | "WHO IS THIS?" + big عَائِلَتِي |

Family members may stand close / arm in arm (they're family); Sami and Lina stay a step apart.
