Letter 1 of 28 — alif: its name, its long «ah» sound, its two shapes, and three words you already know. Its one rule: alif joins the letter before it, never the letter after it.

🔤 Alif:
alif — the letter alif — ⁧ألف⁩
ana — I — ⁧أنا⁩
ahlan — hello — ⁧أهلا⁩
kitaab — book — ⁧كتاب⁩
shaay — tea — ⁧شاي⁩
