Sami meets Lina for the first time: one whole conversation in Arabic, with the English on screen — greetings, names, where you are from, coffee at the café and the way to the market. From Episode 1, the full course from zero.

📝 Some of what you'll learn:
as-salaamu 'alaykum — hello (peace be upon you) — ⁧السَّلَامُ عَلَيْكُمْ⁩
maa smuk? — what is your name? — ⁧مَا اسْمُكْ؟⁩
min ayna anta? — where are you from? — ⁧مِنْ أَيْنَ أَنْتَ؟⁩
uriidu qahwa — I want coffee — ⁧أُرِيدُ قَهْوَةْ⁩
uHibbu dimashq! — I love Damascus! — ⁧أُحِبُّ دِمَشْقْ!⁩
