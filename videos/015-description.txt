Day 15 of 30 — two little words to order anything in an Arabic café, and how to say no politely.

Two minutes a day. Your first Arabic conversation in 30 days.

🆕 New words:
uriidu — I want — ⁧أُرِيدُ⁩
laa uriidu — I don't want — ⁧لَا أُرِيدُ⁩
maa' — water — ⁧مَاءْ⁩
shaay — tea — ⁧شَايْ⁩
'aSiir — juice — ⁧عَصِيرْ⁩

📝 Today's sentences:
uriidu qahwa. — I want coffee. — ⁧أُرِيدُ قَهْوَةْ.⁩
uriidu shaay, min faDlik. — I want tea, please. — ⁧أُرِيدُ شَايْ، مِنْ فَضْلِكْ.⁩
laa, shukran. — No, thanks. — ⁧لَا، شُكْرًا.⁩
laa uriidu qahwa. — I don't want coffee. — ⁧لَا أُرِيدُ قَهْوَةْ.⁩
