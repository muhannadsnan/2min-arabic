Day 9 of 30 — the feminine ending (taa marbuuTa): one little letter turns a man into a woman in Arabic (student, teacher, friend).

Two minutes a day. Your first Arabic conversation in 30 days.

🆕 New words:
taalib / taaliba — student (man / woman) — ⁧طَالِبْ / طَالِبَةْ⁩
mu'allim / mu'allima — teacher (man / woman) — ⁧مُعَلِّمْ / مُعَلِّمَةْ⁩
Sadiiq / Sadiiqa — friend (man / woman) — ⁧صَدِيقْ / صَدِيقَةْ⁩
haziina — sad (woman) — ⁧حَزِينَةْ⁩
Liinaa taaliba — Lina is a student — ⁧لِينَا طَالِبَةْ⁩
hiya mu'allima — she is a teacher — ⁧هِيَ مُعَلِّمَةْ⁩
