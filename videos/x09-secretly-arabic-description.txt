Arabic Extras — you already speak some Arabic: 10 English words that came into English from Arabic, each said by a native speaker.

☕ The 10 words (English — Arabic):
coffee — qahwa — ⁧قهوة⁩
sugar — sukkar — ⁧سكر⁩
lemon — laymuun — ⁧ليمون⁩
cotton — quTn — ⁧قطن⁩
giraffe — zaraafa — ⁧زرافة⁩
safari — safar (journey) — ⁧سفر⁩
zero — Sifr (empty) — ⁧صفر⁩
algebra — al-jabr — ⁧الجبر⁩
magazine — makhaazin (storehouses) — ⁧مخازن⁩
mattress — maTraH (a place to lie down) — ⁧مطرح⁩

Some of them started even earlier in Persian or Sanskrit, but they all reached English through Arabic.
