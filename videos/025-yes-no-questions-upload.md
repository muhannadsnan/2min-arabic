## Description

```
Day 25 of 30 — yes/no questions in Arabic: put one little word, hal, in front of any sentence and it becomes a question.

Two minutes a day. Your first Arabic conversation in 30 days.

🆕 The word:
hal — (turns a sentence into a yes/no question) — ⁧هَلْ⁩

📝 Examples:
anta Taalib. — You are a student. — ⁧أَنْتَ طَالِبْ.⁩
hal anta Taalib? — Are you a student? — ⁧هَلْ أَنْتَ طَالِبْ؟⁩
na'am, ana Taalib. — Yes, I am a student. — ⁧نَعَمْ، أَنَا طَالِبْ.⁩
hal anti min lubnaan? — Are you from Lebanon? — ⁧هَلْ أَنْتِ مِنْ لُبْنَانْ؟⁩
hal tuHibbu l-qahwa? — Do you like coffee? — ⁧هَلْ تُحِبُّ الْقَهْوَةْ؟⁩
hal haadhaa kitaabuk? — Is this your book? — ⁧هَلْ هَذَا كِتَابُكْ؟⁩
laa, haadhaa kitaabii! — No, this is MY book! — ⁧لَا، هَذَا كِتَابِي!⁩
```
