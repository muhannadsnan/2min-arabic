Day 31 — month two starts: questions about YOU. Ask «what's your favorite color?» in Arabic (to a man and to a woman), answer with your own color, and hear Sami and Lina answer.

🎨 Ask and answer:
maa lawnuk al-mufaddal? — What's your favorite color? — ⁧ما لونك المفضل؟⁩
maa lawnuki al-mufaddal? — …to a woman — ⁧ما لونكِ المفضل؟⁩
lawni al-mufaddal al-azraq — My favorite color is blue. — ⁧لوني المفضل الأزرق⁩
uHibbu l-lawn al-akhDar — I love the color green. — ⁧أحب اللون الأخضر⁩
aHmar · akhDar · aSfar · abyaD · aswad — red · green · yellow · white · black — ⁧أحمر · أخضر · أصفر · أبيض · أسود⁩
