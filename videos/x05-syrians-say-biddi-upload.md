## Description

```
Arabic Extras — in the textbook, "I want" is uriidu. In Syria and Lebanon, people say biddi. Here's how to order like a local.

Two minutes a day. Your first Arabic conversation in 30 days — this is a bonus lesson after Day 15.

📘 Standard Arabic (understood everywhere):
uriidu — I want — ⁧أُرِيدُ⁩

🗣️ Syrian dialect (everyday speech in Syria):
biddi — I want — ⁧بِدِّي⁩
biddi ahwe — I want coffee (the q is silent!) — ⁧بِدِّي قَهْوِة⁩
ma biddi — I don't want — ⁧مَا بِدِّي⁩
shu biddak? / shu biddik? — What do you want? (to a man / a woman) — ⁧شُو بِدَّكْ؟ / شُو بِدِّكْ؟⁩
biddi shaay, allah ykhallik — I want tea, please — ⁧بِدِّي شَايْ، الله يْخَلِّيكْ⁩
tikram! — Sure, my pleasure! — ⁧تِكْرَمْ!⁩

🔔 Subscribe so you don't break your streak.

#learnarabic #syrianarabic #2minutearabic
```
