Day 2 of 30 — the 10 Arabic phrases you'll use every single day.

Two minutes a day. Your first Arabic conversation in 30 days.

📝 Today's Arabic:
1. as-salaamu 'alaykum — peace be upon you (hello) — ⁧السَّلَامُ عَلَيْكُمْ⁩
2. wa 'alaykumu s-salaam — and upon you, peace (the reply) — ⁧وَعَلَيْكُمُ السَّلَامْ⁩
3. kayfa haaluk? — how are you? (to a woman: kayfa haaluki?) — ⁧كَيْفَ حَالُكْ؟⁩
4. bikhayr, al-hamdu lillaah — fine, thank God — ⁧بِخَيْرْ، الْحَمْدُ لِلَّهْ⁩
5. shukran — thank you — ⁧شُكْرًا⁩
6. 'afwan — you're welcome — ⁧عَفْوًا⁩
7. min fadlik — please — ⁧مِنْ فَضْلِكْ⁩
8. maa smuk? — what's your name? — ⁧مَا اسْمُكْ؟⁩
9. ismii ... — my name is ... — ⁧اسْمِي ...⁩
10. laa afham — I don't understand — ⁧لَا أَفْهَمْ⁩
