Month 2, Day 4 — ask «what's your hobby?» in Arabic and answer «my hobby is…». Six hobbies: reading, drawing, music, cooking, travel and photography. And Sami's hobby? Cooking… and eating the cooking!

🎨 Ask and answer:
maa hiwaayatuk? — What's your hobby? — ⁧ما هوايتك؟⁩
maa hiya hiwaayatuki? — What's your hobby? (to a woman) — ⁧ما هي هوايتكِ؟⁩
hiwaayatii al-qiraa'a — My hobby is reading. — ⁧هوايتي القراءة⁩
uHibbu r-rasm wa-l-muusiiqaa — I love drawing and music. — ⁧أحب الرسم والموسيقى⁩

📚 6 hobbies:
al-qiraa'a — reading — ⁧القراءة⁩
ar-rasm — drawing — ⁧الرسم⁩
al-muusiiqaa — music — ⁧الموسيقى⁩
aT-Tabkh — cooking — ⁧الطبخ⁩
as-safar — travel — ⁧السفر⁩
at-taSwiir — photography — ⁧التصوير⁩
