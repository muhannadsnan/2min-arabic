## Description

```
Family members in Arabic: father, mother, brother, sister, and the one sound at the end that turns each word into "my". Then Lina shows you her family, said by native speakers.

father — ab — ⁧أب⁩
mother — umm — ⁧أم⁩
brother — akh — ⁧أخ⁩
sister — ukht — ⁧أخت⁩
This is my brother. — haadhaa akhii — ⁧هذا أخي⁩
This is my family. — haadhihi 'aa'ilatii — ⁧هذه عائلتي⁩
This is my father. — haadhaa abii — ⁧هذا أبي⁩
This is my mother. — haadhihi ummii — ⁧هذه أمي⁩

The trick: add a long ee sound (-ii) and the word means "my". For a woman, "this" changes too: haadhaa → haadhihi. Who is in your family? Write one line in Arabic in the comments.
```
