📝 Some of what you'll learn:
as-salaamu 'alaykum — hello — ⁧السَّلَامُ عَلَيْكُمْ⁩
kayfa haaluk? — how are you? — ⁧كَيْفَ حَالُكْ؟⁩
shukran — thank you — ⁧شُكْرًا⁩
maa smuk? — what's your name? — ⁧مَا اسْمُكْ؟⁩
tasharrafnaa — nice to meet you — ⁧تَشَرَّفْنَا⁩
hal turiidu qahwa? — do you want coffee? — ⁧هَلْ تُرِيدُ قَهْوَةْ؟⁩
ana taalib — I am a student — ⁧أَنَا طَالِبْ⁩
laa afham — I don't understand — ⁧لَا أَفْهَمْ⁩
