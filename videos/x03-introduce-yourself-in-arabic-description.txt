🗣️ Your introduction, line by line:
marhaban — Hello — ⁧مَرْحَبًا⁩
ismii … — My name is … — ⁧اسْمِي …⁩
ana min … — I'm from … — ⁧أَنَا مِنْ …⁩
ana Taalib — I'm a student (man) — ⁧أَنَا طَالِبْ⁩
ana Taaliba — I'm a student (woman) — ⁧أَنَا طَالِبَةْ⁩
adrusu l-'arabiyya. wa-anta? / wa-anti? — I study Arabic. And you? — ⁧أَدْرُسُ الْعَرَبِيَّةْ⁩
tasharrafnaa — Nice to meet you — ⁧تَشَرَّفْنَا⁩
