# Day 15 — Phrases: I Want / I Don't Want

| | |
|---|---|
| **Format** | Phrases (with a short café scene: the waiter offers coffee, Sami orders tea) |
| **Target length** | ~2:15 |
| **Goal** | The viewer orders with أُرِيدُ + a drink + مِنْ فَضْلِكْ, says "I don't want" by putting لَا in front, and connects أُرِيدُ to Lina's هَلْ تُرِيدُ قَهْوَةْ؟ from Day 4. |
| **New words** | أُرِيدُ (I want) · لَا أُرِيدُ (I don't want) · مَاءْ (water) · شَايْ (tea) · عَصِيرْ (juice) |
| **Re-used (due)** | قَهْوَةْ · هَلْ تُرِيدُ قَهْوَةْ؟ (Day 4) · مِنْ فَضْلِكْ (Day 2) · لَا، شُكْرًا · طَيِّبْ (Day 6) · أُمِّي (Day 13, warm-up) |
| **Voices** | English narrator = owner's clone · Arabic = the owner (teacher, the waiter's lines, Sami) + Koki (Lina, Day 4 line) → **AI label: No** |
| **Look** | 3D · the café and the waiter from Day 10 · opener: the waiter, close-up, holds up a small glass of tea beside his face with raised eyebrows and a warm smile ("tea?") and holds (new opener, owner approves) |
| **YouTube title** | `Order Anything in Arabic With These 2 Words \| Day 15 · 2 Minute Arabic` |
| **Thumbnail text** | "2 WORDS" + big أُرِيدُ |

Words stay in their simple pausal form (أُرِيدُ شَايْ) — the case endings (قَهْوَةً) wait for later.
