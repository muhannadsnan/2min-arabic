## Description

```
Day 19 of 30 — Sami gets lost in Damascus. Learn to ask for directions in Arabic: right, left, then, here and there.

Two minutes a day. Your first Arabic conversation in 30 days.

🆕 New words:
yamiin — right — ⁧يَمِينْ⁩
yasaar — left — ⁧يَسَارْ⁩
thumma — then — ⁧ثُمَّ⁩
hunaa — here — ⁧هُنَا⁩
hunaak — there — ⁧هُنَاكْ⁩

📖 The story:
saamii fii dimashq. — Sami is in Damascus. — ⁧سَامِي فِي دِمَشْقْ.⁩
'afwan, ayna l-maqhaa? — Excuse me, where is the café? — ⁧عَفْوًا، أَيْنَ الْمَقْهَى؟⁩
yamiin? yasaar? — Right? Left? — ⁧يَمِينْ؟ يَسَارْ؟⁩
yamiin, thumma yasaar. — Right, then left. — ⁧يَمِينْ، ثُمَّ يَسَارْ.⁩
hunaa yaa saamii! anaa hunaa! — Here, Sami! I'm here! — ⁧هُنَا يَا سَامِي! أَنَا هُنَا!⁩
```
