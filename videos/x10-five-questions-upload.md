## Description

```
Basic Arabic questions and answers — the 5 questions every Arab asks a visitor in the first minutes, and the easy answer formula for each, said by a native speaker.

🗣️ The 5 questions (English — how to say it — Arabic):
What's your name? — maa ismuk? — ⁧ما اسمك؟⁩
→ My name is … — ismi … — ⁧اسمي …⁩
Where are you from? — min ayna anta? — ⁧من أين أنت؟⁩
→ I'm from … — ana min … — ⁧أنا من …⁩
How old are you? — kam 'umruk? — ⁧كم عمرك؟⁩
→ I'm … years old — 'umri … sana — ⁧عمري … سنة⁩
What's your job? — maa 'amaluk? — ⁧ما عملك؟⁩
→ I'm a student — ana taalib — ⁧أنا طالب⁩
Do you speak Arabic? — hal tatakallam al-'arabiyya? — ⁧هل تتكلم العربية؟⁩
→ Yes, a little — na'am, qaliilan — ⁧نعم، قليلا⁩

Fill in your own name, country, age and job. The best last answer is just two words: na'am, qaliilan.
```
