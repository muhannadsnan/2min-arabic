# Day 3 — Your First Arabic Conversation

| | |
|---|---|
| **Format** | Conversation |
| **Target length** | ~2:05 |
| **Goal** | The viewer understands a full greeting conversation and can play Sami's part. |
| **New words** | تَشَرَّفْنَا (*tasharrafnaa* — nice to meet you) · وَأَنْتِ؟ (*wa anti?* — and you? — to a woman) |
| **Voices** | narrator + teacher + Sami = owner's clone · Lina = Sara's clone → **AI label: Yes** |
| **YouTube title** | `Understand a Real Arabic Conversation After 2 Days \| Day 3 · 2 Minute Arabic` |
| **Thumbnail text** | "YOU'LL UNDERSTAND THIS" + Sami & Lina |

Markup: see [docs/05-style-guide.md](../docs/05-style-guide.md). Each `say:` block = one audio clip.
Images and their references: `images/003-first-conversation/prompts.json`.

**Structure:** hook → your hello → today → the conversation line by line (Arabic, then meaning; the two new words
repeated by the teacher) → role-play (Lina speaks, the viewer answers as Sami, answer hint on the card) → outro → goodbye.

**Setting:** Sami and Lina meet for the first time in a small, cozy café.
