# Day 3 — Your First Arabic Conversation

| | |
|---|---|
| **Format** | Conversation |
| **Target length** | ~2:20 |
| **Goal** | The viewer understands a full greeting conversation and can play Sami's part. |
| **New words** | تَشَرَّفْنَا (*tasharrafnaa* — nice to meet you) · وَأَنْتِ؟ (*wa anti?* — and you? — to a woman) |
| **YouTube title** | `Understand a Real Arabic Conversation After 2 Days \| Day 3 · 2 Minute Arabic` |
| **Thumbnail text** | "YOU'LL UNDERSTAND THIS" + Sami & Lina |

Markup: see [docs/05-style-guide.md](../docs/05-style-guide.md). Each `say:` block = one audio clip.

**Structure:** 1) Listen & understand (each line in Arabic, then its meaning) → 2) Role-play (Lina speaks, the viewer answers as Sami).

> Optional if you have room (or for the long-form weekly compilation): play the full dialogue once *before* the line-by-line part, Arabic only, no translation.

**Setting:** Sami and Lina meet for the first time at a small café. One image per line is ideal; at minimum
use the three images listed (wide shot / close-ups / goodbye) and alternate close-ups of whoever is speaking.

### 🎬 Scene 4 — Listen & understand, line by line · 0:25–1:25

🖼️ **Image:** Alternate close-ups of Sami and Lina, whoever is speaking (from the character sheets) + STYLE
🔤 **On screen:** each line in Arabic · transliteration · English (see table below)
✂️ **Edit:** small "NEW" sticker on lines 4 and 6.

| # | Who | Arabic | Transliteration | English |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Lina | السَّلَامُ عَلَيْكُم. | *as-salaamu 'alaykum.* | Peace be upon you. |
| 2 | Sami | وَعَلَيْكُمُ السَّلَام. | *wa 'alaykumu s-salaam.* | And upon you, peace. |
| 3 | Lina | أَنَا لِينَا. مَا اسْمُك؟ | *ana Liinaa. maa smuk?* | I'm Lina. What's your name? |
| 4 | Sami | اسْمِي سَامِي. تَشَرَّفْنَا. | *ismii Saamii. tasharrafnaa.* | My name is Sami. Nice to meet you. **NEW** |
| 5 | Lina | تَشَرَّفْنَا. كَيْفَ حَالُك؟ | *tasharrafnaa. kayfa haaluk?* | Nice to meet you. How are you? |
| 6 | Sami | بِخَيْر، الْحَمْدُ لِلَّه. وَأَنْتِ؟ | *bikhayr, al-hamdu lillaah. wa anti?* | Fine, thank God. And you? **NEW** |
| 7 | Lina | بِخَيْر، شُكْرًا. | *bikhayr, shukran.* | Fine, thanks. |
| 8 | Sami | مَعَ السَّلَامَة. | *ma'a s-salaama.* | Goodbye. |
| 9 | Lina | مَعَ السَّلَامَة. | *ma'a s-salaama.* | Goodbye. |

### 🎬 Scene 5 — Role-play: you are Sami · 1:25–1:55

🖼️ **Image:** Lina facing the camera directly (first-person view, as if the viewer is Sami), smiling + STYLE
🔤 **On screen:** 🎭 You are Sami — answer Lina! (show Sami's line as a hint *after* each pause)

```say:narrator

```
```say:lina

```
⏸️ **Pause 3s** — viewer: *wa 'alaykumu s-salaam.*
```say:lina
أَنَا لِينَا. مَا اسْمُك؟
```
⏸️ **Pause 3s** — viewer: *ismii …* (their own name) *tasharrafnaa.*
```say:lina
تَشَرَّفْنَا. كَيْفَ حَالُك؟
```
⏸️ **Pause 3s** — viewer: *bikhayr, al-hamdu lillaah. wa anti?*
```say:lina
بِخَيْر، شُكْرًا. مَعَ السَّلَامَة.
```
⏸️ **Pause 2s** — viewer: *ma'a s-salaama.*
