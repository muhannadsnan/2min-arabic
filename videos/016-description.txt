Day 16 of 30 — order your coffee exactly how you like it in Arabic: with milk, without sugar, and ask how much it is.

Two minutes a day. Your first Arabic conversation in 30 days.

🆕 New words:
tafaDDal / tafaDDalii — here you go (to a man / a woman) — ⁧تَفَضَّلْ / تَفَضَّلِي⁩
ma'a — with — ⁧مَعَ⁩
biduun — without — ⁧بِدُونِ⁩
bikam haadhaa? — how much is this? — ⁧بِكَمْ هَذَا؟⁩

📝 The café order:
ahlan wa sahlan! — Welcome! — ⁧أَهْلًا وَسَهْلًا!⁩
uriidu qahwa ma'a Haliib, min faDlik. — I want coffee with milk, please. — ⁧أُرِيدُ قَهْوَةْ مَعَ حَلِيبْ، مِنْ فَضْلِكْ.⁩
laa, biduun sukkar. — No, without sugar. — ⁧لَا، بِدُونِ سُكَّرْ.⁩
shukran. bikam haadhaa? — Thank you. How much is this? — ⁧شُكْرًا. بِكَمْ هَذَا؟⁩
khamsa — five — ⁧خَمْسَةْ⁩
