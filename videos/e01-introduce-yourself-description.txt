Say your name, ask someone theirs, and tell people where you are from in Arabic — for a man and for a woman — then hear Sami and Lina do it. From Episode 1, the full course from zero.

📝 Some of what you'll learn:
maa smuk? — what is your name? (to a man) — ⁧مَا اسْمُكْ؟⁩
maa smuki? — what is your name? (to a woman) — ⁧مَا اسْمُكِ؟⁩
ismii… — my name is… — ⁧اسْمِي…⁩
min ayna anta? — where are you from? (to a man) — ⁧مِنْ أَيْنَ أَنْتَ؟⁩
ana min suuriyaa — I am from Syria — ⁧أَنَا مِنْ سُورِيَا⁩
ana min lubnaan — I am from Lebanon — ⁧أَنَا مِنْ لُبْنَانْ⁩
