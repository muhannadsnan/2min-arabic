📝 Some of what you'll learn:
ahlan wa sahlan — welcome — ⁧أَهْلًا وَسَهْلًا⁩
dimashqu jamiila — Damascus is beautiful — ⁧دِمَشْقُ جَمِيلَةْ⁩
kitaabii / baytii — my book / my house — ⁧كِتَابِي / بَيْتِي⁩
ab, umm, akh, ukht — father, mother, brother, sister — ⁧أَبْ، أُمّ، أَخْ، أُخْتْ⁩
haadhihi — this (for a woman) — ⁧هَذِهِ⁩
uriidu shaay, min faDlik — I want tea, please — ⁧أُرِيدُ شَايْ، مِنْ فَضْلِكْ⁩
