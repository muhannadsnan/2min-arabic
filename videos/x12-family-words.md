```say:teacher

```

🖼️ **Image:** sister (pencil)
🔤 **On screen:** أخت · *ukht* · sister · 4/4

### 🎬 Scene 6 — the "my" ending

🖼️ **Image:** same as scene 4
🔤 **On screen:** أخي · *akhii* · my brother (brother + ee)

```say:narrator

```
```say:teacher
هَذَا أَخِي.
```
⏸️ **Pause 1.0s**
