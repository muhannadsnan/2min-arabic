Arabic Vocab — 10 parts of the body in Arabic, from your head down to your heart. Point and say each one.

Two minutes a day. Your first Arabic conversation in 30 days — this is a vocabulary bonus.

🧍 The 10 body words:
ra's — head — ⁧رَأْسْ⁩
sha'r — hair — ⁧شَعْرْ⁩
'ayn — eye — ⁧عَيْنْ⁩
udhun — ear — ⁧أُذُنْ⁩
anf — nose — ⁧أَنْفْ⁩
fam — mouth — ⁧فَمْ⁩
yad — hand — ⁧يَدْ⁩
rijl — leg / foot — ⁧رِجْلْ⁩
Sadr — chest — ⁧صَدْرْ⁩
qalb — heart — ⁧قَلْبْ⁩
