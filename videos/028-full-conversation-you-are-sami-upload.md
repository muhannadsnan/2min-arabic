Day 28 of 30 — your first full Arabic conversation, from hello to goodbye. Lina asks, you answer in the pause, then hear Sami.

💬 The conversation:
marHaban! — Hello! — ⁧مَرْحَبًا!⁩
ahlan! — Hi! — ⁧أَهْلًا!⁩
kayfa Haaluk? — How are you? — ⁧كَيْفَ حَالُكْ؟⁩
bikhayr, al-Hamdu lillaah. wa-anti? — Fine, thank God. And you? — ⁧بِخَيْرْ، الْحَمْدُ لِلَّهْ. وَأَنْتِ؟⁩
bikhayr. maa smuk? — Fine. What's your name? — ⁧بِخَيْرْ. مَا اسْمُكْ؟⁩
ismii saamii. — My name is Sami. — ⁧اسْمِي سَامِي.⁩
min ayna anta? — Where are you from? — ⁧مِنْ أَيْنَ أَنْتَ؟⁩
ana min suuriyaa. — I'm from Syria. — ⁧أَنَا مِنْ سُورِيَا.⁩
hal anta Taalib? — Are you a student? — ⁧هَلْ أَنْتَ طَالِبْ؟⁩
na'am, ana Taalib. — Yes, I'm a student. — ⁧نَعَمْ، أَنَا طَالِبْ.⁩
hal turiidu qahwa? — Do you want coffee? — ⁧هَلْ تُرِيدُ قَهْوَةْ؟⁩
na'am, uriidu qahwa ma'a Haliib! — Yes, I want coffee with milk! — ⁧نَعَمْ، أُرِيدُ قَهْوَةْ مَعَ حَلِيبْ!⁩
ma'a s-salaama! — Goodbye! — ⁧مَعَ السَّلَامَةْ!⁩
ilaa l-liqaa'! — See you! — ⁧إِلَى اللِّقَاءْ!⁩
