HOW TO READ: slowly and clearly, like a teacher. ~2 s silence between lines, don't say the numbers. Indoors, phone close.
Misspoke? Pause and say the whole line again — the last take wins. Voice Memos → send as a DOCUMENT.
Lines marked (Sami) = say them as Sami, a bit younger and lighter. Everything in English is made from your voice clone.
You can record it in one go, or one part at a time (each part = one file).
