### 🎬 Scene 4 — My story · 0:25–0:55

🖼️ **Image A (0:25–0:35):** A person at night surrounded by notebooks full of notes, headphones on, laptop playing a video, very motivated, Spanish flag colors subtly in the room + STYLE
🖼️ **Image B (0:35–0:45):** The same notebooks now covered in dust on a shelf, a calendar with many pages flying away, the person busy on the phone in the background + STYLE
🖼️ **Image C (0:45–0:55):** The same person sitting at a café table talking a little nervously with a smiling local, simple speech bubbles without text + STYLE
🔤 **On screen:** 🇪🇸 Spanish = my 4th language · 2 years · A2 level

```say:narrator
Arabic is my first language. Spanish is my fourth — and I've been learning it for two years. But I learned it the wrong way. One month, I'd go all in: notes, videos, podcasts, hours and hours of conversations. Then life got busy, and I wouldn't touch Spanish for two months. Then another burst. Then another break. Two years later, I can finally hold a simple conversation with a native speaker. Two years — for level A2.
```

### 🎬 Scene 5 — The realization · 0:55–1:05

🖼️ **Image:** A calendar where every single day has a small green check mark, a tiny stopwatch icon on each day + STYLE
🔤 **On screen:** 2 minutes × every day > 1 month × sometimes

```say:narrator
And here's the painful part. If I had given Spanish just two minutes, every single day, I'd be much further today. Two minutes isn't magic. Every day is.
```

### 🎬 Scene 6 — The 1% math · 1:05–1:25

🖼️ **Image:** Two paths from the same starting point: one staircase gently rising to a bright mountain top, the other slowly sinking into fog; a small figure at the start + STYLE
🔤 **On screen (appear one by one):** 1.01³⁶⁵ ≈ **37.8** 📈 · 0.99³⁶⁵ ≈ **0.03** 📉
✂️ **Edit:** pop the first number in on "thirty-eight", the second on "close to zero".

```say:narrator
Here's the math. Get one percent better every day, and after one year you're not three hundred sixty-five percent better. You're about thirty-eight times better. One point zero one, to the power of three hundred sixty-five. Now slip one percent every day, and after a year you're close to zero. Small things, done daily, compound.
```

### 🎬 Scene 7 — The promise · 1:25–1:37

🖼️ **Image:** A hand holding a phone with a stopwatch at 2:00, a sunrise behind it + STYLE
🔤 **On screen:** 2 min/day → your first Arabic conversation in 30 days

```say:narrator
So don't make my mistake. Don't wait for the perfect month — it never comes. Give me two minutes a day, and in thirty days you'll start your first conversation in Arabic. No BS. No excuses.
```
