## Description

```
Arabic Extras — what does habibi really mean? Word by word it's "my love" (Hubb = love + the little "my" from Day 12). And who do Arabs really say it to? The one you love, children, and the family you love. Between grown-up friends: only as a joke.

💛 The word:
Habibee — my love, my dear (to a man) — ⁧حَبِيبِي⁩
Habibtee — my love, my dear (to a woman) — ⁧حَبِيبْتِي⁩
Hubb — love — ⁧حُبّ⁩

👨‍👧 How Arabs really use it:
ta'aal Habibee — Come here, sweetie. (a father to his son) — ⁧تَعَالْ حَبِيبِي⁩
naamii Habibtee — Sleep, sweetheart. (to a little girl) — ⁧نَامِي حَبِيبْتِي⁩
shwayy shwayy Habibee — Slowly, slowly, sweetie. (a big brother) — ⁧شْوَيّ شْوَيّ حَبِيبِي⁩
```
