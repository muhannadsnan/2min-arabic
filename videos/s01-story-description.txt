An easy Arabic short story for beginners: Rami the horse is fast, Maher the turtle is slow, and both go to the market. So how does the turtle arrive first? Listen to the whole story, answer easy questions about every sentence, learn 5 pairs of opposites, then hear the same story in the past and in the future. Arabic on screen, English translation for every line.

By the end, you can tell this story yourself — in three tenses.

📝 Words from the story:
yadh-hab — goes — ⁧يذهب⁩
yamshii — walks — ⁧يمشي⁩
yaSil — arrives — ⁧يصل⁩
yaraa — sees — ⁧يرى⁩
yashrab — drinks — ⁧يشرب⁩
yastamirr — keeps going — ⁧يستمر⁩
saree' ↔ baTii' — fast ↔ slow — ⁧سريع ↔ بطيء⁩
qabl ↔ ba'd — before ↔ after — ⁧قبل ↔ بعد⁩
ma'a ↔ biduun — with ↔ without — ⁧مع ↔ بدون⁩
yanaam ↔ yastayqiZ — sleeps ↔ wakes up — ⁧ينام ↔ يستيقظ⁩
hunaa ↔ hunaak — here ↔ there — ⁧هنا ↔ هناك⁩
