## Description

```
Day 12 of 30 — Arabic has no word for "my". You add one sound to the end of the word instead.

Two minutes a day. Your first Arabic conversation in 30 days.

🆕 The endings:
-ii — my — ⁧ـِي⁩
-uka — your (to a man) — ⁧ـُكَ⁩
-uki — your (to a woman) — ⁧ـُكِ⁩

📝 Examples:
ismii — my name — ⁧اسْمِي⁩
kitaabii — my book — ⁧كِتَابِي⁩
baytii — my house — ⁧بَيْتِي⁩
qahwatii — my coffee (the soft "a" becomes "t") — ⁧قَهْوَتِي⁩
ismuka / ismuki — your name (man / woman) — ⁧اسْمُكَ / اسْمُكِ⁩
haadhaa kitaabii! — This is my book! — ⁧هَذَا كِتَابِي!⁩

▶️ Start from Day 1: https://youtu.be/XTQXhypYDLU
🔔 Subscribe so you don't break your streak.

#learnarabic #arabicgrammar #2minutearabic
```
