```
Arabic Extras — 10 ways to say goodbye in Arabic: 5 in standard Arabic (fusha), 5 the way Syrians really say it.

Two minutes a day. Your first Arabic conversation in 30 days — this is a bonus lesson.

📘 Standard Arabic (understood everywhere):
1. ma'a s-salaama — Goodbye (go with safety) — ⁧مَعَ السَّلَامَةْ⁩
2. ilaa l-liqaa' — Until we meet again — ⁧إِلَى اللِّقَاءْ⁩
3. araaka laahiqan — See you later (to a man) — ⁧أَرَاكَ لَاحِقًا⁩
4. ilaa l-ghad — Until tomorrow — ⁧إِلَى الْغَدْ⁩
5. fii amaani llaah — In God's protection — ⁧فِي أَمَانِ اللهْ⁩

🇸🇾 Syrian dialect (everyday speech in Syria):
6. bkhaatrak — Bye (said by the one leaving) — ⁧بْخَاطْرَكْ⁩
7. allaah ma'ak — God be with you (the reply) — ⁧اللهْ مَعَكْ⁩
8. mnshuufak ba'dein — See you later — ⁧مْنْشُوفَكْ بَعْدَيْن⁩
9. yalla baay — OK, bye — ⁧يَلَّا بَايْ⁩
10. tisbah 'ala khayr — Good night (wake up to goodness) — ⁧تِصْبَحْ عَلَى خَيْرْ⁩

▶️ Start the 30-day course from Day 1: https://youtu.be/XTQXhypYDLU
🔔 Subscribe so you don't break your streak.

#learnarabic #syrianarabic #2minutearabic
```
