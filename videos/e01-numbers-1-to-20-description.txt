Count from 1 to 20 in Arabic, read the Arabic digits you see on prices and buses, and learn the one easy pattern behind 13–19. From Episode 1, the full course from zero.

📝 Some of what you'll learn:
waaHid — one — ⁧وَاحِدْ⁩
ithnaan — two — ⁧اِثْنَانْ⁩
thalaatha — three — ⁧ثَلَاثَةْ⁩
khamsa — five — ⁧خَمْسَةْ⁩
'ashara — ten — ⁧عَشَرَةْ⁩
aHada 'ashar — eleven — ⁧أَحَدَ عَشَرْ⁩
ithnaa 'ashar — twelve — ⁧اِثْنَا عَشَرْ⁩
'ishruun — twenty — ⁧عِشْرُونْ⁩
