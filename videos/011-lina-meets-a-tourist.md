# Day 11 — Conversation: Lina Meets a Tourist

| | |
|---|---|
| **Format** | Conversation (a tourist stops Lina on a Damascus street) |
| **Target length** | ~2:20 |
| **Goal** | The viewer follows a whole 8-line street conversation, can welcome someone (أَهْلًا وَسَهْلًا), say "I'm a tourist", and says "Damascus is beautiful" — using the Day 5 rule (no "is") and the Day 9 ending (a city is feminine). |
| **New words** | أَهْلًا وَسَهْلًا (welcome) · سَائِحْ (tourist) · عَفْوًا (excuse me — a new use of "you're welcome") · دِمَشْقْ (Damascus) · جَمِيلَةْ (beautiful, feminine) |
| **Re-used (due)** | أَنَا مِنْ + countries (Day 8) · كَيْفَ حَالُكْ؟ · شُكْرًا (Day 2) · تَشَرَّفْنَا (Day 3) · numbers (Day 10, warm-up) |
| **Voices** | English narrator = owner's clone · Arabic = the owner (teacher, Tom) + Koki (Lina) → **AI label: No** |
| **Look** | 3D · new character **Tom** (young American tourist: light-brown short hair, light-blue shirt, beige trousers, a backpack) · opener: Tom, close-up on a Damascus street, raises one hand politely ("excuse me") with a friendly smile and holds (new opener, owner approves) |
