# Day 10 — Numbers 1 to 10 (the Coffee Counting Game)

| | |
|---|---|
| **Format** | Numbers + counting game (the café waiter brings coffee) |
| **Target length** | ~2:15 |
| **Goal** | The viewer can count from 1 to 10, recognise numbers out of order, and notices that 3–10 end in the soft "a" from Day 9. |
