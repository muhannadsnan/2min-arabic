Arabic Extras — count from 11 to 20 in Arabic. Eleven and twelve are special, 13–19 follow one easy pattern, and twenty is a new word.

Two minutes a day. Your first Arabic conversation in 30 days — this is a bonus lesson after Day 10 and the Arabic digits.

🔢 Numbers 11–20:
aHada 'ashar — 11 — ⁧١١ أَحَدَ عَشَرْ⁩
ithnaa 'ashar — 12 — ⁧١٢ اِثْنَا عَشَرْ⁩
thalaathata 'ashar — 13 — ⁧١٣ ثَلَاثَةَ عَشَرْ⁩
arba'ata 'ashar — 14 — ⁧١٤ أَرْبَعَةَ عَشَرْ⁩
khamsata 'ashar — 15 — ⁧١٥ خَمْسَةَ عَشَرْ⁩
sittata 'ashar — 16 — ⁧١٦ سِتَّةَ عَشَرْ⁩
sab'ata 'ashar — 17 — ⁧١٧ سَبْعَةَ عَشَرْ⁩
thamaaniyata 'ashar — 18 — ⁧١٨ ثَمَانِيَةَ عَشَرْ⁩
tis'ata 'ashar — 19 — ⁧١٩ تِسْعَةَ عَشَرْ⁩
'ishruun — 20 — ⁧٢٠ عِشْرُونْ⁩
