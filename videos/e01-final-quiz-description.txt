Eight questions from a whole month of beginner Arabic: answer out loud before you hear the answer. If you get most of them, you can already have a real first conversation. From Episode 1, the full course from zero.

📝 Some of what you'll learn:
ismii saamii — my name is Sami — ⁧اسْمِي سَامِي⁩
kayfa Haaluk? — how are you? — ⁧كَيْفَ حَالُكْ؟⁩
uriidu shaay, min faDlik — I want tea, please — ⁧أُرِيدُ شَايْ، مِنْ فَضْلِكْ⁩
dimashqu jamiila — Damascus is beautiful — ⁧دِمَشْقُ جَمِيلَةْ⁩
