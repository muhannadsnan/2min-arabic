# Day 19 — Story: Sami gets lost (and Lina was there all along)

| | |
|---|---|
| **Format** | Story (5 direction words first, then a 6-beat story with a twist) |
| **Target length** | ~2:10 |
| **Goal** | The viewer asks "where is the café?", understands right · left · then · here · there, and follows a short story told only in Arabic lines. |
| **New words** | يَمِينْ (right) · يَسَارْ (left) · ثُمَّ (then) · هُنَاكْ (there) |
