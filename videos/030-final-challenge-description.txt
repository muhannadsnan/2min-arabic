Day 30 of 30 — the final challenge: a stranger at the café asks you six real questions. Answer in the pause, then hear Sami. Thirty days, two minutes a day: this is your first Arabic conversation.

❓ The 6 questions:
marHaban! kayfa Haaluk? — Hello! How are you? — ⁧مَرْحَبًا! كَيْفَ حَالُكْ؟⁩
maa smuk? min ayna anta? — What's your name? Where are you from? — ⁧مَا اسْمُكْ؟ مِنْ أَيْنَ أَنْتَ؟⁩
hal tatakallamu l-'arabiyya? — Do you speak Arabic? — ⁧هَلْ تَتَكَلَّمُ الْعَرَبِيَّةْ؟⁩
hal turiidu qahwa? — Do you want coffee? — ⁧هَلْ تُرِيدُ قَهْوَةْ؟⁩
mataa d-dars? — When is the lesson? — ⁧مَتَى الدَّرْسْ؟⁩
mumtaaz! ma'a s-salaama! — Excellent! Goodbye! — ⁧مُمْتَازْ! مَعَ السَّلَامَةْ!⁩

🆕 New today:
qabla — before (the opposite of after) — ⁧قَبْلَ⁩
mabruuk! — Congratulations! — ⁧مَبْرُوكْ!⁩
