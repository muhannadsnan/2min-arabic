Arabic Vocab — 10 food words you'll need on your first day in an Arab country, each on a real dish from a Damascus market, and your first sentence with them.

🍞 The 10 foods:
khubz — bread — ⁧خُبْزْ⁩
aruzz — rice — ⁧أَرُزّ⁩
laHm — meat — ⁧لَحْمْ⁩
dajaaj — chicken — ⁧دَجَاجْ⁩
samak — fish — ⁧سَمَكْ⁩
jubn — cheese — ⁧جُبْنْ⁩
bayD — eggs — ⁧بَيْضْ⁩
khuDaar — vegetables — ⁧خُضَارْ⁩
fawaakih — fruit — ⁧فَوَاكِهْ⁩
shuurba — soup — ⁧شُورْبَةْ⁩

📝 Your first sentence:
aakulu khubz. — I eat bread. — ⁧آكُلُ خُبْزْ.⁩
