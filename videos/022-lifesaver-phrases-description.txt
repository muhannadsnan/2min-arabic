Day 22 of 30 — three Arabic lifesavers for when someone speaks too fast: slowly, please · how do I say … in Arabic? · repeat, please.

Two minutes a day. Your first Arabic conversation in 30 days.

🛟 The lifesavers:
bi-buT', min faDlik — Slowly, please. — ⁧بِبُطْءْ، مِنْ فَضْلِكْ.⁩
kayfa aquulu … bil-'arabiyya? — How do I say … in Arabic? — ⁧كَيْفَ أَقُولُ … بِالْعَرَبِيَّةْ؟⁩
a'id, min faDlik — Repeat, please. — ⁧أَعِدْ، مِنْ فَضْلِكْ.⁩
maadhaa ya'nii …? — What does … mean? — ⁧مَاذَا يَعْنِي …؟⁩

💬 In the café:
laa afham. bi-buT', min faDlik. — I don't understand. Slowly, please. — ⁧لَا أَفْهَمْ. بِبُطْءْ، مِنْ فَضْلِكْ.⁩
hal turiidu qahwa? — Do you want coffee? — ⁧هَلْ تُرِيدُ قَهْوَةْ؟⁩
