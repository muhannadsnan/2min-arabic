Arabic Vocab — 20 jobs in Arabic and the question every Arab asks right after your name, plus how to answer it.

❓ The question:
maa 'amaluk? — What do you do? — ⁧ما عملك؟⁩

💼 The 20 jobs:
Taalib — student — ⁧طالب⁩
mu'allim — teacher — ⁧معلم⁩
Tabiib — doctor — ⁧طبيب⁩
mumarriD — nurse — ⁧ممرض⁩
muhandis — engineer — ⁧مهندس⁩
muHaasib — accountant — ⁧محاسب⁩
mubarmij — programmer — ⁧مبرمج⁩
mudiir — manager — ⁧مدير⁩
muwaZZaf — office worker — ⁧موظف⁩
mutarjim — translator — ⁧مترجم⁩
muHaamii — lawyer — ⁧محامي⁩
muSawwir — photographer — ⁧مصور⁩
Tabbaakh — cook — ⁧طباخ⁩
khabbaaz — baker — ⁧خباز⁩
najjaar — carpenter — ⁧نجار⁩
Hallaaq — barber — ⁧حلاق⁩
saa'iq — driver — ⁧سائق⁩
Tayyaar — pilot — ⁧طيار⁩
fallaaH — farmer — ⁧فلاح⁩
shurTii — police officer — ⁧شرطي⁩

📝 Your answer:
ana Tabiib. — I'm a doctor. — ⁧أنا طبيب.⁩
ana Tabiiba. — I'm a doctor. (woman) — ⁧أنا طبيبة.⁩
A woman adds one letter at the end, like on Day 9 — ⁧ة⁩
