☕ The 10 café words:
qahwa — coffee — ⁧قَهْوَةْ⁩
shaay — tea — ⁧شَايْ⁩
maa' — water — ⁧مَاءْ⁩
Haliib — milk — ⁧حَلِيبْ⁩
sukkar — sugar — ⁧سُكَّرْ⁩
finjaan — a coffee cup — ⁧فِنْجَانْ⁩
ka'ka — cake — ⁧كَعْكَةْ⁩
'aSiir — juice — ⁧عَصِيرْ⁩
naadil — waiter — ⁧نَادِلْ⁩
Hisaab — the bill — ⁧حِسَابْ⁩
