Day 8 of 30 — how to ask "where are you from?" in Arabic, and how to answer with your country.

Two minutes a day. Your first Arabic conversation in 30 days.

🆕 New words:
min ayna anta? — where are you from? (to a man) — ⁧مِنْ أَيْنَ أَنْتَ؟⁩
min ayna anti? — where are you from? (to a woman) — ⁧مِنْ أَيْنَ أَنْتِ؟⁩
ana min … — I am from … — ⁧أَنَا مِنْ⁩
Suuriyaa — Syria — ⁧سُورِيَا⁩
Lubnaan — Lebanon — ⁧لُبْنَانْ⁩
Amriikaa — America — ⁧أَمْرِيكَا⁩
BariiTaaniyaa — Britain — ⁧بَرِيطَانِيَا⁩
Almaaniyaa — Germany — ⁧أَلْمَانِيَا⁩
al-Hind — India — ⁧الْهِنْدْ⁩
