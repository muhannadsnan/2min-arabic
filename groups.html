English: Copy text → Open the Reel → Share → click Group at the bottom (don't type in the first box, don't press "Share now") → pick the group → paste into "Create a public post…" → Post.

Other languages: open the group → Create post → Photo/video → pick the file from Downloads → fb-groups → paste → Post.

About a minute each. Every day has its own 5 (the same on every device); post them 10–15 minutes apart. Skipped ones come back in the next round.
