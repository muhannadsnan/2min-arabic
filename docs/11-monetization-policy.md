# 11 — YouTube Monetization Policies, and How This Channel Complies

Source: [YouTube channel monetization policies](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/1311392?hl=en)
(read 2026-09-29). Summary of what matters, then a check of our channel against each rule.
Thresholds and the 2027 changes are in [07-channel-and-monetization.md](07-channel-and-monetization.md).

## The rules in short

A monetized channel must follow **all** of: Community Guidelines, Terms of Service, copyright, AdSense policies,
advertiser-friendly content guidelines, and the monetization policies below. Content must be **original and authentic**.

### 1. Inauthentic content (renamed from "repetitious content", July 2025)
- **Not allowed:** content that is **repetitive or mass-produced** and feels "interchangeable from video to video";
  a "highly similar storyline template" with the same outcome; "image slideshows, templated storylines, or scrolling
  text with minimal or no narrative"; "AI-generated content made with generic or unoriginal templates" without the
  creator's authentic perspective.
- **Allowed:** consistent intros/outros **when the core content is substantially different**; a series with recurring
  characters **when each episode has a distinct storyline, focus or concept**; content that delivers creative or
  **educational value**.

### 2. Reused content
- **Not allowed:** repurposing content already on YouTube or elsewhere **without significant original commentary,
  substantive modifications, or educational value**; compilations of others' clips with little narrative; content
  copied without substantive changes; readings of material you didn't create; pitch/speed-changed songs.
- **Allowed:** clips used for critical review or commentary; edited footage where you add a storyline and commentary;
  content where the uploader is visible or explains their contribution.
- Reviewers judge the **whole channel**; a violation can remove monetization from the entire channel.

### 3. Unsatisfying or off-putting content
Not allowed: emotionally manipulative formulas, shock for views, stitched-together unrelated AI clips, deceptive imagery.
Allowed: a cohesive storyline and "your unique creative voice", including when AI tools are used.

### 4. AI personas on sensitive topics
AI-generated "experts" on health, legal, financial or political topics cannot be monetized.

### 5. Kids and family content quality
Low-quality "made for kids" content (promotional, deceptive "educational", confusing, sensational) is demonetized.

### 6. Creator integrity
No artificial inflation of views, subscribers, likes, watch time or ad impressions; no deceptive or fraudulent use of
monetization features. Violations → suspension or termination across **all** of the creator's channels.

### Enforcement and review
YouTube can withhold earnings for up to 90 days, limit ads per video, suspend monetization or terminate a channel.
Reviewers look at the **channel theme, most-viewed videos, newest videos, watch-time share, and metadata**
(titles, thumbnails, descriptions).

## Our channel against each rule

| Rule | Our risk | Why we pass / what we do |
|---|---|---|
| **Inauthentic content** | **Medium — the one to watch** | Fixed intro/outro is explicitly allowed because **every lesson is different** (new words, new format: phrases / conversation / story / grammar / quiz) and builds on a 30-day curriculum. The host is **a real person on camera** (Day 1, hello and goodbye in every video); **all Arabic is recorded by real people** (the owner and Koki); scripts are original. **Keep it that way:** never publish two lessons that feel interchangeable, film real on-camera moments regularly (e.g. every Part video), keep the personal voice ("my mistake with Spanish", "most learners…"). |
| AI-generated visuals / narration | Low–medium | AI illustrations and the owner's **own** cloned English voice are tools, not the content: each video carries a real teaching narrative, real voices for the language, and the creator's perspective. Avoid anything that looks like a bare slideshow — every scene has a teaching card and purpose. |
| **Reused content** (Part compilations) | Low | Compilations re-use **our own** lessons **plus substantive new material**: a new review quiz, restructured flow (repeated intros/outros removed), chapter and word panels, new narration. **Every Part must keep adding something new** (quiz at minimum). **Never** use other people's footage or stock clips (the watermarked iStock clip was rejected for this reason). |
| Unsatisfying content | None | Calm, cohesive lessons; no shock or clickbait. Thumbnails and titles must match the content. |
| AI personas on sensitive topics | None | Language teaching is not health/legal/finance/politics, and the host is a real person. |
| Kids content | None | Audience set to **not made for kids**; content is for adult learners. |
| Creator integrity | None if we keep it clean | **Never** buy views/subscribers, never use sub-for-sub or engagement groups, never ask people to click ads. Asking viewers to like, comment or subscribe is fine. |
| Copyright | Low | Our own recordings, our own generated images (Apache-licensed model), YouTube Audio Library music only, own subscribe animation. |
| Advertiser-friendly | None | Educational, family-safe language. |

## Before applying for monetization (checklist)

- [ ] The **newest and most-viewed** videos are the strongest ones (reviewers look there first).
- [ ] Titles, thumbnails and descriptions are honest and match each video (no keyword stuffing, no hashtags in titles).
- [ ] At least a few **on-camera** videos exist besides Day 1 (strengthens "authentic creator").
- [ ] Channel description explains who the host is and what the course is.
- [ ] No reused third-party content anywhere on the channel.
- [ ] AdSense account linked, 2-Step Verification on (see [07](07-channel-and-monetization.md)).
