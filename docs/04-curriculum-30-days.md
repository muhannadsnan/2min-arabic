# 04 — The 30-Day Curriculum

**Goal (the promise):** after Day 30, a beginner can hold a *first* real conversation:
greet, introduce themselves, ask and answer "how are you / where are you from / what's your name",
say what they want, handle "I don't understand / slower please", and say goodbye.

Everything below is built backwards from that goal. Each week ends with a review day.
Days 1–5 are fully scripted in [../videos/](../videos/). The rest is a plan — adjust as you learn
what the audience responds to.

## After Day 30

- **Month 2 (Days 31–60):** A1 consolidation — past tense basics, food, shopping, directions, time.
- Keep the **Day N** counter going; it's the channel's identity (Day 100, Day 365 are big milestone videos).
- Weekly **compilation** long-form video (all 7 days back to back) for the long-form tab and search.
- Community-driven topics: pin a comment "What do you want to be able to say?" and build days from it.
