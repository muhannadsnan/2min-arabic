| Product | Contents | Type |

2 Minute Arabic, for as long as it is published" — promise content access, never personal teaching. Mention it in one
line at the end, the bio, a pinned comment and promo posts — never a pitch inside a 2-minute lesson.

## Payments and tax (Claude's notes, 20 Oct — not legal advice)

- **Income tax follows tax residency, not the bank's country.** Living in Norway = taxable in Norway, wherever the
  money sits; moving it to a low-tax country's bank to avoid Norwegian tax would be tax evasion. Legal ways to pay less:
  register an ENK (sole proprietorship) and deduct real costs (equipment, software, internet, home-office share).
  Talk to a Norwegian accountant before the first sale.
- **Stripe** needs a bank account in the business's country (Norway is supported); it holds the balance and pays out
  on a schedule (manual possible) — a holding balance, not a bank. ~3 % + fixed fee per sale, but VAT for every buyer
  country is then the seller's job.
- **Merchant of record (recommended): Lemon Squeezy (owned by Stripe) or Paddle** — they are the legal seller, handle
  VAT everywhere (Norway's VOEC, EU OSS), regional prices and refunds; ~5 % + $0.50 per sale; pay out to a bank or
  PayPal. Check current fees when it's time.

archive holds several months of material. Then compare cost per sale by tier, not cost per follower.
