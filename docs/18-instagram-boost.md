## Our additions (data from 20 Oct)

- **Which Reel:** **Day 3** "Sami meets Lina: your first full Arabic conversation" — most saves (66) and shares (13),
  1,803 views, and its topic is the series' promise. Runner-up Day 5 (1,858 views, 50 saves). The owner added
  "Follow for a new Arabic lesson every day" to both captions (20 Oct).
- **Dubbed Reels change the "drop" list:** Turkey, Uzbekistan, Kazakhstan, Azerbaijan, Afghanistan were dropped for
  English-only Reels. We have dubbed versions (Turkish; Russian — widely understood in Central Asia and Azerbaijan;
  Persian/Dari) → a small boost per language with the dubbed Reel in that language. Check in the app whether a Trial
  Reel must be "Shared to profile" before it can be boosted.
- **Quran / Islamic studies interests:** our lessons teach spoken Modern Standard Arabic for conversation, not Quranic
  reading — followers from those interests may leave. Test separately and small, or leave out.
- **Before spending:** bio ("A new 2-minute Arabic lesson every day · YouTube 👇"), 3 pinned posts (Day 1, Day 3, the
  best Extra) — they decide the visit → follow rate.

## Measure after the boost

Cost per follower · profile visit → follow rate · watch time of the boosted Reel · follower countries a week later ·
unfollows after 7 days · clicks to YouTube.
