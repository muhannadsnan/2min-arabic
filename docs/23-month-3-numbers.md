# 23 — Month 3: the numbers month (owner idea 2026-11-09, plan for his OK)

**Idea (owner):** numbers give endless videos — first the counting ladder (1 digit, 2 digits, under 50, under 100,
bigger), then numbers used in subjects: daily and simple (prices, time, phone numbers), in between (weights, height,
distances) and "statistical / geographical / scientific" (the Arab world in numbers, the body in numbers).
"Numbers using subjects and subjects using numbers."

**Why it should work:** numbers + lists are our strongest proven format (Day 10: 73 % viewed) and among the most
searched beginner topics (suggest.py 9 Nov: "arabic numbers 1 to 100 / 1 to 20 / 1 to 50 / 1 to 1000 / 100 to 200",
"numbers in arabic 1-100"). Many of them are also life questions with personal answers (how tall are you, what year
were you born, how many brothers) → comments ([[platform taste]] in plan-lessons).

**When:** after Month 2 (≈ mid-December → mid-January). Seasonal Extras still go in between (Happy New Year late
December, Ramadan phrases mid-January — plan-lessons §6). The Month 2 weekly rhythm stays: course slots
(Mon/Wed/Fri) + Sunday vocab carry the numbers; Tue/Thu stay letters; Saturday = long video.

## Rules for the numbers month
- **Grammar stays light.** Number + noun agreement is the hardest part of Arabic (3–10 take the opposite gender
  ending, 11–99 take a singular noun). Teach **whole chunks** that are correct (ثلاثة كتب، ثلاث سيارات, عشرون
  ليرة) instead of the rules. At most one "surprise" Short shows that the ending flips; it's never drilled.
- **Recording:** all Arabic is the owner's. Compound numbers (خمسة وعشرون) are recorded whole, never stitched from
  separate number clips (choppy). One long sheet can cover a week. The 1–100 long video = one straight recording
  (~6 min).
- **Re-use:** each video's quiz re-uses due numbers from earlier ones (spacing, plan-lessons §2).
- **Visuals:** in the new drawing style if it's started by then ([[2min-naive-style-switch]]); one clear object per
  number subject (price tag, clock, scale, map).
