# Monthly story video (owner 2026-11-03)

The extension to the monthly Episode: Episode N teaches the month's lessons; this video **re-uses the month's best
material in a new shape** — a mini-story with animals, comprehension questions, the key words, then the same story in
the past and in the future. 16:9 long video (long videos bring ~3× the subscribers per view of Shorts: Parts 3.1 vs
Shorts 0.9 per 100 views, Oct 2026). 3–4 Shorts are cut from it to promote it and fill days.

## Updated 2026-11-03 (owner) — the signature lives in .claude/skills/story-episode/SKILL.md
Drawing-book watercolour pictures (centre detailed, fading to white paper), words popping up in the white space,
full story read first then sentence-by-sentence with many easy logical questions, word PAIRS (a word + its opposite),
5 chiming stars. Story 1 = the owner's text: رامي الحصان، ماهر السلحفاة، مازن الثعلب، هاني الخروف، كامل العصفور
(recording sheet: videos/_s01-whatsapp.txt). The draft below is superseded.

## Rules (owner)
- Entirely different organisation and **new pictures** from the lessons: generated still images (not animation), one
  per story sentence, the same animal characters throughout. Clean animals only (horse, sheep, camel, fox, turtle,
  rabbit, birds…; no dogs, no pigs).
- **Arabic on screen without tashkeel** (beginner text), only where needed to avoid a wrong reading.
- The narrator is shown: sometimes alone, full screen (a presenter close-up talking to the camera), sometimes in a
  small square while a story picture + its sentence fill the screen.
- A strong hook, no boring stretches: every section ≤ 3–4 min, a change of view every few seconds.

## Shape (≈ 12–15 min)
1. **Hook (0–20 s)** — the ending first: the turtle at the market, the horse asleep. "A turtle beats a horse — and by the
   end you'll tell this story in Arabic, in the past and in the future."
2. **The story (present)** — 10 sentences, each: picture + Arabic + translit + English, read by the owner; then the
   whole story once more with Arabic only.
3. **Questions** — after each part of the story, 1–2 questions in Arabic in different shapes (who…? what…? where…?
   with whom…? is it X or Y? does X…?), pause, answer.
4. **Words to keep** — 5–6 words of the month that appear in the story: what it means, when, where and with whom
   you use it, 2 real examples.
5. **The story in the past** — the same pictures; each changed verb highlighted (يذهب → ذهب).
6. **The story in the future** — سـ + the verb (سيذهب), a few sentences.
7. **Outro** — "tell the story yourself in the comments", subscribe, Episode / Day 1 links.

## Shorts from it (3–4, ≈ 45–60 s, vertical)
the story (present) · "Can you answer these 3 questions?" · present → past ("one sound changes everything") · the future.
