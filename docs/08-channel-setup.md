## Step 0 — Safety checks (10 min, once per Google account)

### 0.1 Turn on 2-Step Verification
1. Open [myaccount.google.com/security](https://myaccount.google.com/security) (signed in as mnd.senan@gmail.com).
2. **How you sign in to Google → 2-Step Verification** → turn on.
3. Add at least two methods: **Google prompt on your phone** + **authenticator app** or **passkey**.
   Save the **backup codes** somewhere safe (password manager).

All channels on this Google account are protected by it (and YPP requires it).

### 0.2 Check the two old channels for strikes
For **each** old channel (profile picture → *Switch account* → pick the channel):
1. Go to [studio.youtube.com](https://studio.youtube.com) → **Settings → Channel → Feature eligibility**.
   - All three levels (Standard / Intermediate / Advanced) available or "Eligible" → no active Community Guidelines strikes.
   - A strike shows up there as features being blocked, and on the **Dashboard** as a violation card.
2. **Content** (left menu) → look at the **Restrictions** column: "Copyright" or "Community Guidelines" entries mean a claim/strike on that video.
3. Also search your Gmail for emails from YouTube about "strike", "Community Guidelines" or "copyright".

Community Guidelines strikes expire after 90 days, so old inactive channels are almost always clean.
Copyright **claims** (not strikes) on old gaming videos are harmless.

## Step 2 — Profile picture and banner (make it human)

### Profile picture — use a real photo of you
Faceless logos look like AI farms. A real, friendly face is the fastest trust signal, and you'll be on camera in Day 1 anyway.
- Head and shoulders, **smiling, looking at the camera**, daylight from a window, plain background.
- Optional touch: holding your phone showing a **2:00** timer.
- Square, at least **800×800 px**. It's shown as a small circle, so crop close to the face.
- Upload: Studio → **Customization → Profile → Picture**.

### Banner
- Size **2560×1440 px**; everything important inside the center **1235×338 px** (the part visible on phones).
- Content: `2 Minute Arabic` · `Two minutes a day. Your first Arabic conversation in 30 days.` · `New lesson every day`
  plus one Arabic word, e.g. مَرْحَبًا.
- Claude can generate it as a PNG from your photo (clean typography, no AI-looking art).
- Upload: Studio → **Customization → Profile → Banner image**.

## Step 5 — Unlock features

Studio → **Settings → Channel → Feature eligibility:**
- **Intermediate features → Verify phone number.** Unlocks custom thumbnails and **videos longer than 15 minutes**
  (needed for the weekly compilations).
- **Advanced features:** unlocks automatically after some channel history, or faster with video verification or ID.
  Needed for **clickable links in descriptions** and the channel's Links section.

## Step 7 — First uploads

- Build a buffer of **5–7 finished videos** before the first one goes public.
- Upload as **Private → Schedule** (Studio → Create → Upload videos): one per day, same hour
  (evening in your target audience's time zone, e.g. 18:00 CET / 12:00 ET).
- On each: title, description, tags from the script file · add to the playlist · upload `captions.srt` as English subtitles ·
  "Altered or synthetic content": **No** (illustrations + your voice/generic voice, nothing realistic-fake).
