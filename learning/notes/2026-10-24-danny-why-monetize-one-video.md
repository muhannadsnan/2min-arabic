## What we change
- **Pre-upload gate (process, applied):** "stranger check" — every Arabic word the narrator says has been introduced on a
  card before (or is shown with its meaning).
- **Proposal (format → owner):** end on the peak. The last 15 s become: the payoff (score / Sami's sentence) → share hook
  → subscribe + goodbye clip; drop "That's your two minutes… same place, two minutes" when the "next lesson" line is there.
