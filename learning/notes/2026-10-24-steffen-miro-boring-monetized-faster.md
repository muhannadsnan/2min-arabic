## What we change

  the only lever for YPP, so long, searchable videos matter more than more Shorts. Proposal: one **long searched video a
  month** (e.g. "Learn Arabic in 30 Minutes — the first 15 days", Parts merged with a new quiz = added value) on top of
  the weekly Part; and keep income from our own products (docs/19, free PDF → paid PDF/course) as the main money plan.
- Topic transfer added to the monthly outlier scan (plan-lessons).
- 80/20 rule written into plan-lessons.
