## Claims (timestamps)
1. **[00:25] Videos are *pulled* for each viewer, not pushed.** Ranking = viewer personalization + video performance.
2. **[01:28] Performance signals:** do viewers choose to watch or ignore / "not interested"; **average view duration and
   average percent viewed**; **satisfaction** (surveys, likes).
3. **[02:02] No favoured format** — the system follows the audience.
4. **[02:21] Changing title/thumbnail can re-rank a video** (because viewers respond differently). Change
   underperformers; **don't change what works**.
5. **[02:51] One weak video doesn't hurt the channel.**
6. **[03:51] Daily/weekly uploads are not required** — growth isn't correlated with time between uploads; avoid burnout.
7. **[04:53] Reach depends on topic interest, competition, seasonality** → Google Trends (filter: YouTube Search).
8. **[06:10] Tags: "not very important"** — mainly for common misspellings.
